Lesson 7, Part 2 – English in Action

Grade 7 – Unit 2: Animals – Opinions, reasons and stressed syllables

You are live on the Animal Talk Show! Interview the guests about cats, lizards, parrots and wild animals. Choose any channel to start – you can play the stages in any order!

👩‍🏫 Teacher's lesson plan (25 minutes)

Lesson aims: to practise saying what you like and don't like and giving a reason; to practise stressed syllables in words.
Target language: I'm crazy about…; I find … really interesting; I quite like…; I don't mind…; I'm not keen on…; I can't stand…; I don't like … at all. Reasons: They're clean and very quiet. / I don't like their fur.
Vocabulary: adjectives + animals (a dangerous tiger, an unusual jellyfish, an unfriendly gorilla…).
Teacher's Book: Lesson 7, part 2, page 117 – Activity Book, Lesson 7 part 2 (audio 2.16, 2.17, 2.18).
